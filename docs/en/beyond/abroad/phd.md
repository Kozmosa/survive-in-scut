# Overseas PhD Programs

An introduction to applying for direct-entry PhD and other doctoral programs abroad from the perspective of a SCUT student.

## Interviews and personal experiences

- [US PhD applications and university experiences in China and the US — Experience Field (in Chinese)](https://experience-field.pages.dev/interviews/us-phd-application/)

This interview, compiled by 在路上的小凉, follows a master's student in the US preparing to apply for PhD programs. It covers building relationships with supervisors, research experience, recommendation letters, and university experiences in China and the US. The interview took place before the application season; the interviewee had not yet submitted applications or received any offers. Read it as one person's experience of preparing to apply.
