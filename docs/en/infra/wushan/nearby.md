# Around Wushan Campus

Wushan Campus sits in the heart of Guangzhou, with the bustling Tianhe District right outside the campus gates and a rich choice of dining, shopping, and entertainment nearby.

## Dining

- **Around Wushan Station**: exit through the East Gate or the East Side Gate to reach the area around Wushan Station, which is packed with all kinds of restaurants and snack shops
- **Around Tianhe Coach Terminal**: exit through the North Area North Gate next to Boxue Building, and a walk of a few hundred meters brings you to Tianhe Coach Terminal, with shopping areas such as Tianhe Xintiandi nearby and plenty of dining options

## Rentals

**Xixiu Village** inside Wushan Campus has staff housing available for rent, though prices are relatively high. Students with relevant experience are welcome to add more rental advice for the surrounding area.

## Transit Connections

### Metro

- **Wushan Station (Line 3)**: the most convenient metro station for the West, South, and East areas, reached by exiting through the East Gate or the East Side Gate
- **Tianhe Coach Terminal (Line 3/Line 6)**: the most convenient metro station for the North Area; exit through the North Area North Gate and walk a few hundred meters

### Cross-Campus Transit

The most convenient and inexpensive way to get to University Town Campus and Guangzhou International Campus is the **campus shuttle bus**. Buses depart from stops such as the on-campus Library and reach University Town Campus and the International Campus via expressways.

- **Travel time**: about 45~60 minutes from Wushan to the University Town Concert Hall; the International Campus falls within the same range, and morning and evening rush hours may make trips longer
- **Frequency**: more departures on weekdays than on weekends

## Leisure & Entertainment

- **Tianhe Xintiandi**: near Tianhe Coach Terminal, with a shopping mall, a cinema, and other facilities.
