# Overview of Dorm E

## 1. Hardware

### a. Dorm Room Interior

Although the rooms are not large, the facilities are quite new as the building was recently constructed.

![Dorm Room Panoramic Overview](/assets/hemc/dorm_e_overview.jpg)

**Basic Specifications:**

- Rooms are generally **5m × 3.6m** four-person dorms
- Loft bed with desk underneath, each person has their own wardrobe, bookshelf, and luggage rack

**Desk:**

![Desk and Storage Cabinet](/assets/hemc/dorm_e_desk.jpg)

- Dimensions: **1.2m × 0.6m**
- One side connects to the loft bed ladder, the other side connects to the wardrobe
- A bookshelf is located at the rear upper portion — plan your space wisely
- Height difference between the bottom of the bookshelf and the desk is **0.6m**

**Wardrobe:**

- Dimensions approximately **0.8m × 0.6m × 1.15m**
- Below is a storage cabinet of the same width and length: **0.8m × 0.6m × 0.45m**
- Four open shelves beside the wardrobe (3 on top measuring **0.3m × 0.25m × 0.33m** + one at the bottom measuring **0.3m × 0.25m × 0.65m**)

**Right-Side Storage:**

- Drawers and cabinets for daily necessities and personal items
- Drawer: **0.4m × 0.33m × 0.1m**
- Cabinet: **0.6m × 0.33m × 0.45m**

**Bookshelves:**

- Three bookshelves overhead, each **0.4m × 0.2m** (open top)

**Ladder Cabinet:**

- The ladder to the loft bed on the left/right side has two built-in storage compartments
  - **0.5m × 0.65m × 0.25m**
  - **0.5m × 0.4m × 0.25m**
- Since two people share one ladder, it's recommended to discuss usage with your roommate

**Bed:**

- Dimensions: **1.9m × 0.9m**
- The school will automatically install safety rails and a mosquito net frame

### b. Balcony, Toilet & Shower

- Balcony is typically **1.8m × 2.15m** (some rooms have a corner occupied by a wall section)
- Includes a washbasin and washing rack
- Toilet and shower are **dry-wet separated**, each roughly half the balcony size
- Water rate: **3.75 CNY/ton**
- Electricity rate: **0.6295 CNY/kWh**

## 2. Amenities

- Dorm rooms come with **air conditioning** and **electric fans**
- **Water dispensers** are located at fixed positions on each floor
- **No mandatory lights-out or power cutoff** (campus network does cut off, though)
- Each building has approximately **7 elevators**
- **E2/E3** have underground parking garages with direct stair access (one elevator to the garage)
- **Self-service vending machines** and **food delivery pickup points** are located downstairs (campus canteen delivery orders or Xiya Supermarket orders can be delivered here)
- Each building has its own unique **functional rooms and activity rooms** downstairs, for example:
  - **E2**: Kitchen, calligraphy room
  - **E3**: Psychological counseling room

## 4. Advice

No matter how nice the dorm is, what really matters is the **people**. Learn how to get along with your roommates, try to be as understanding as possible, and plan out responsibilities clearly.

Hope you have a wonderful stay in University Town's Dorm E!
