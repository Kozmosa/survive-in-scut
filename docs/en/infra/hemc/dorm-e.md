# Area E Dorms Overview

## 1. Hardware

### a. Inside the Dorm

The room itself is not large, but since the buildings are newly built, the facilities are all fairly new.

![Full view of the dorm](/assets/hemc/dorm_e_overview.jpg)

**Basic specs:**

- Dorms are generally four-person rooms measuring **5m × 3.6m**
- Lofted beds with desks underneath, plus your own wardrobe, bookshelf, and luggage rack

**Desk:**

![Desk and storage cabinets](/assets/hemc/dorm_e_desk.jpg)

- Size: **1.2m × 0.6m**
- One side connects to the stairs of the lofted bed, the other to the wardrobe
- There is a bookshelf at the back on top, so plan the space wisely
- The height gap between the bottom of the bookshelf and the desk is **0.6m**

**Wardrobe:**

- Roughly **0.8m × 0.6m × 1.15m**
- Below it sits a storage cabinet with the same width and length: **0.8m × 0.6m × 0.45m**
- Next to the wardrobe are four open cubbies (three on top measuring **0.3m × 0.25m × 0.33m** plus one at the very bottom measuring **0.3m × 0.25m × 0.65m**)

**Right-side storage:**

- Drawers and cabinets for daily necessities and personal items
- Drawers: **0.4m × 0.33m × 0.1m**
- Cabinet: **0.6m × 0.33m × 0.45m**

**Book cabinets:**

- Three book cabinets overhead, each **0.4m × 0.2m** (open at the top)

**Staircase storage:**

- The stairs up to the bed are on the left/right side, with two storage compartments built into them
  - **0.5m × 0.65m × 0.25m**
  - **0.5m × 0.4m × 0.25m**
- Since two people share one staircase, agree with your roommate on how to use them

**Bed:**

- Size: **1.9m × 0.9m**
- The school automatically installs guardrails and a frame for hanging a mosquito net

### b. Balcony, Toilet, and Shower

- The balcony is usually **1.8m × 2.15m** (in some dorms a corner is taken up by a wall)
- There is a washbasin counter and a wash rack
- The toilet and shower have a **wet/dry separation**, each roughly half the size of the balcony
- Water: **3.75 RMB/ton**
- Electricity: **0.6295 RMB/kWh**

## 2. Software

- Each dorm comes with **air conditioning** and a **fan**
- **Water dispensers** are at fixed spots on every floor
- **Lights and power stay on all night** (the campus network does cut out)
- Each building should have **7 elevators**
- **e2/e3** have an underground garage with stairs leading straight down (plus one elevator that goes to the garage)
- Downstairs there are **vending machines** and **food-delivery pickup points** (delivery from the campus canteens or Xiya Supermarket can be sent here)
- Each building has its own **functional rooms and activity rooms**, for example:
  - **e2**: kitchen, calligraphy room
  - **e3**: psychological counseling room

## 4. Advice

However nice the dorm is, what matters most is the **people**. Learn how to get along with your roommates, be as accommodating to one another as you can, and sort out responsibilities in advance.

Hope you have a wonderful time living in Area E at University Town!
