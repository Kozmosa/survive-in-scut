# GZIC Guide

## Study & Library Resources

- **E3 Central Library**: The main library of the International Campus, equipped with study rooms and reading areas, offering a rich book collection and plenty of study seats
- **D1 Wu Xianming Intelligent Engineering College Library**: A small library inside the college

---

## Daily Service Points

Daily services on the International Campus are concentrated **near McDonald's (the area between Buildings D5 and F5)**:

- **Food & drink**: McDonald's, Luckin Coffee, Meet Noodles, Huayuan Yunli
- **Others**: gym, print shop, hair salon

---

## Sports & Venues

The International Campus has one gymnasium and one swimming hall. Students who know about their opening hours, booking methods, and fees are welcome to add the details.

## Dining & Convenience Facilities

### Cafeterias

- **F5 Cafeteria**: Mixue and a baozi shop on the first floor, Cotti Coffee on the second floor
- **D5 Cafeteria**: —
- **B2 Cafeteria**: Serves Luosifen (river snail rice noodles) and grilled meat rice, and has a small campus store

## GZIC-Specific Facilities

- **Undergraduate dormitories**: concentrated in Building D5 (residential college housing)

## Sports Grounds

- **North Gate track field** and **Southwest Gate track field**: the two track and field grounds on campus
