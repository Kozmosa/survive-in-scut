# Around GZIC

## Dining

- **Around the Southwest Gate**: A few minutes' walk down the alley from the Southwest Gate brings you to the **Nancun shopping area**, with plenty of restaurants along the way and inside the area
- **Around the North Main Gate**: Turn right outside the North Main Gate to find Shaxian Delicacies and other restaurants
- **Along the way to Banqiao Station**: B Ji Fish Congee (open in the evenings only) and other shops

## Daily Shopping

The Nancun shopping area has supermarkets and fruit shops, enough to meet everyday needs. There is no wet market nearby for now.

## Rentals

Students with relevant experience are welcome to add rental information for the area around the International Campus.

## Transit Connections

- **Banqiao Station (Line 7)**: relatively close, reachable on foot or by shared bike
- **Xinzao Station (Line 4)**: reachable by shared bike, but farther away

## Leisure & Entertainment

The Nancun shopping area has KTV, a cinema, and other facilities. Entertainment options around the International Campus are limited; for more choices, head into the city.
