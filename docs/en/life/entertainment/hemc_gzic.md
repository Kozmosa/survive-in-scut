## Finding Rhythm Game Arcades

[Nearcade, the global rhythm game arcade map](https://nearca.de/), is developed by SCUT alumnus [Naptie](https://github.com/Naptie). It covers arcade locations worldwide across major titles such as maimai DX, CHUNITHM, Taiko no Tatsujin, and beatmania IIDX, lets you search for arcades near a university (including SCUT campuses) or within a custom radius, and offers campus arcade-density rankings and club communities — handy for planning where to play before heading out.

[GitHub Repository Link](https://github.com/Naptie/nearcade)
