# Academic Calendar

Below is the week grid for the current semester. It automatically highlights the **current week** and **today**, and shows the teaching-week progress and holiday countdowns.

::: warning Subject to official notices
This calendar is maintained by hand and may lag behind the latest university arrangements. Holidays and schedule changes follow the official notices from the university. If you spot outdated data, feel free to [contribute](/en/others/contributing) an update.
:::
