## Final Review Tools

If your review time is limited and your materials are scattered, you can use [Exam Hacker](https://github.com/Wh1te358/skills-by-Wh1te/tree/codex/exam-hacker-skill/exam-hacker) to organize textbooks, lecture slides, past exam papers, and notes into a target-score-oriented review plan.

This Skill is mainly used to evaluate target scores, compress core concepts, reverse-engineer and break down past exam papers, map out the knowledge chains behind comprehensive questions, and generate A4 review outlines, helping you judge priorities and make strategic trade-offs within limited time.
