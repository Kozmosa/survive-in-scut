## Sports & Venues

### Daoming Swimming Hall

* **Location**: inside Wushan Campus
* **Type**: heated indoor swimming hall
* **Lanes**: the practice area and the competition area are separate, each with 4 or more lanes
* **Facilities**: showers, lockers, and other basic amenities
* **Fees**: discounts for SCUT students
* **Booking & entry**: handled through the WeChat mini program "Hengsheng Sports Venues" (恒生体育场馆)

## Dining & Convenience Facilities

### Canteens

* **West District Canteen**: serves morning tea (dim sum) and has round tables for large group meals
* **Central District Canteen**: counters for siu mei (Cantonese roast meats), fast food, and more
* **South District Canteen**: —
* **North Canteen 1**: its siu mei is famous throughout SCUT
* **North Canteen 2**: has a Yihetang drinks shop
* **Fadiansuo Restaurant**: more refined dishes at relatively higher prices

### On-Campus Supermarket

* **Shengjia Supermarket**: near North Canteen 2
