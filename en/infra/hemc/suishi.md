# Suishi Village

Suishi Village sits right next to University Town Campus — walk straight along the road between the Library and the A1 teaching building for about 3 minutes and you reach it right outside the school gate. It is a great place for everyday meals out and shopping.

## Dining

* **Noodles/rice**: Zhenya Beef Noodles, Longjiang Pork Knuckle Rice
* **Full meals/group dinners**: Beidiaor'er Copper Pot Chicken, Northeastern Chinese food, Wuhan stir-fry, Xianmiao Roast Chicken, chicken pot (jigongbao)
* **Drinks**: Mixue, ChaPanda, Lucky Cup

## Daily Shopping

Suishi Village has privately run supermarkets slightly larger than the on-campus Xiya Xing'an Comprehensive Supermarket, but no large chain supermarkets like RT-Mart or Walmart. Everyday fruit, snacks, and sundries can all be bought here.

## Transit Connections

To return to campus from Suishi Village, enter through the **East Gate**. Getting to the metro station requires crossing the entire teaching area, so compared with going to University Town South Station from West Gate 1 of the living area, Suishi Village is not particularly convenient for metro access.

## Rentals

Suishi Village has rooms available for student renters, suitable for postgraduate exam preparation, internships, and similar situations. Students with relevant experience are welcome to add detailed rental advice.
